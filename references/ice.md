# ICE prioritization

Score each opportunity 1–10:

- **Impact** — effect on the client's main KPI (leads, conversion rate, CAC) in that market.
- **Confidence** — strength of the evidence: the benchmark already does it and it's visible (high), best practice (medium), hunch (low).
- **Ease** — effort to ship: copy change (high), new page / tracking (medium), new channel or partnership (low).

ICE = (I + C + E) / 3. Sort descending.

## Selection rules
- **Top 3 quick wins per market**: highest ICE with Ease ≥ 7.
- **1 cross-market bet**: highest Impact affecting at least 2 markets, even if Ease is low.
- Every opportunity links to the gap it closes (dimension + market + evidence).
- If two opportunities are the same action in different markets, merge them and list the markets.

## Opportunity format
| # | Market | Dimension | Opportunity | I | C | E | ICE | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 30-day test plan format
| Week | Test | Hypothesis | Channel | KPI | Tracking needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Hypothesis format: "If we [change], then [KPI] will [direction], because [evidence]."
Tracking needed: name the event, the tool (GA4, Looker Studio, CRM) and the UTM convention.
