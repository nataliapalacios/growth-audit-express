# Growth Audit Express — Ironhack vs Le Wagon (FR · ES · UK)

*Draft v0.1 · Audit date: 25 Oct 2026 · Client: Ironhack · Benchmark: Le Wagon*
*Unit of comparison: Data Analytics bootcamp + public-funding entry funnel*

---

## 1. Executive summary

- **Ironhack wins Spain by paying for it.** ~58 active Meta ads, SEPE-funded free courses, ISA and scholarships. Le Wagon runs zero Meta ads in Spain and is absent from ChatGPT's Madrid answer.
- **Ironhack loses France and the UK, where Le Wagon wins without paid social.** Le Wagon has zero Meta ads in all three markets, yet ranks #1 in London and #2 in Paris in ChatGPT. Its levers: French public funding (CPF, France Travail, RNCP level 6) and a strong organic and review footprint.
- **The Spanish playbook does not travel.** In France, Ironhack's 3 ads are in English, CPF and France Travail are not named on program pages, and ChatGPT warns that "sessions may be remote and in English". In the UK: 0 ads, absent from AI answers, prices in euros and CET.
- **Highest-ICE action:** fix the price and duration inconsistencies on the Spanish AI landing (ICE 8.3). It costs one afternoon on Ironhack's highest-traffic market.
- **Cross-market bet:** export the public-funding GTM to France (CPF / France Travail) and the UK (Skills Bootcamps).

## 4. Key findings by market

**France — the gap is localization, not budget**
1. Ironhack advertises in France in English (the "Hiring Shift" webinar). *So what:* a French career-changer sees an English ad for an English course. Le Wagon gets this audience without ads.
2. Ironhack's French financing page (ironhack.com/fr/option-de-financement) lists only upfront payment (€750 deposit), 3 or 6 interest-free instalments and Quotanda (up to 24 months). No CPF, France Travail, OPCO or RNCP. Le Wagon makes public funding its core argument. *So what:* in France, "is it CPF-eligible?" is often the first qualifying question, and Ironhack has no answer.
3. ChatGPT already repeats Ironhack's language weakness. *So what:* the AI channel is amplifying the problem, and fixing the pages fixes the answer.

**Spain — Ironhack leads, but leaks conversion**
1. The AI on-demand landing says 25% off in the form and -20% in pricing, with four price points (€799 / €990 / €66 / €83) and the 40h duration only in the meta title. *So what:* friction at the moment of purchase on the newest product.
2. The SEPE funnel promises "documents → form → 15-min interview". In mystery shopping (Oct 12), the lead was first asked to complete an unannounced logic test before any call. *So what:* expectation gap, so likely drop-off between sign-up and call.
3. The SEPE landing declares locale `en_US` on a Spanish page, and the saved remote page shows no hreflang (to verify live). *So what:* cheap technical SEO fixes on high-intent pages.

**UK — Ironhack is dormant**
1. 0 Meta ads, absent from ChatGPT's London answer.
2. The /gb pages show euros, CET start times and "anyone joining from anywhere in Europe".
3. ChatGPT cites a competitor's government-funded cohort (iO-Sphere). Neither Ironhack nor Le Wagon mentions UK Skills Bootcamps funding.

## 7. Method & limits

- Sources: public websites (25 Oct 2026), Meta Ad Library (active ads, FR/ES/UK), one ChatGPT run per market in the local language, one mystery-shopping sign-up (ES, Oct 2026, personal data anonymized).
- Not checked: Google Ads Transparency Center, Perplexity and Gemini, Search Console data, Ironhack CPF eligibility on Mon Compte Formation. A Le Wagon "1" in Paid means no Meta ads, not no paid activity.
- Paid data are signals only: counts, themes and formats. No spend estimates.
- AI answers vary between runs, so the GEO scores are a directional sample.
